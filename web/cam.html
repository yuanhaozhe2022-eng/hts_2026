Webcam taps (experimental)

Hold the phone like a wheel in front of the laptop, thumbs resting along its top edge, where the webcam can see them. Lift a thumb and tap it back down on the edge: left thumb = shift down, right thumb = shift up. Video stays on this computer; only "tap left/right" goes to PocketWheel.

Then click into BeamNG: the game only reads the controller while it's the focused window. Opened with python main.py --cam, this window keeps watching while it's behind the game; an ordinary browser tab pauses itself there.
