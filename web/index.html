PocketWheel

Hold your phone sideways like a steering wheel.
Left thumb: brake & shift down. Right thumb: throttle & shift up.

🔒

iPhone: turn on Rotation Lock first. Open Control Center and tap the lock button. PocketWheel turns itself sideways, so the screen can't flip mid-corner.

Tip: on iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen for full screen.

Vibration motors (phone mount)

Force feedback (motor base)

Not connected to the PC.

Pulls the wheel to straight. Used in BeamNG, and in Assetto Corsa / ACC as much as Game force is turned down.

The game's own self-aligning force, which follows the car's path. 0% turns it off and uses the centering spring instead.

Raise it if the wheel wobbles or overshoots center.

Experimental: shift without buttons

Hold the phone like a wheel, press Learn, then tap the back with a finger as asked. Taps on the screen don't count.

Uses the rear camera (asks permission) and more battery. Needs some light to see the finger.

Custom controls

Extra on-screen controls: buttons (a controller button or keyboard key), joysticks (camera, walking, or WASD / arrow keys) and axes (one stick axis or a trigger). Place them with Edit layout; ⚙ sets each one's click, mode, color and feel.
