PhoneWheel

Hold your phone sideways like a steering wheel.
Left thumb: brake & shift down. Right thumb: throttle & shift up.

🔒

iPhone: turn on Rotation Lock first. Open Control Center and tap the lock button. PhoneWheel turns itself sideways, so the screen can't flip mid-corner.

Tip: on iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen for full screen.
